How did we get here

The short version

Five turns that decided what I do with my days.

2019

Back to elementary school

Health issues pulled me out of gymnasium. The school I returned to happened to teach extended IT — the luckiest bad break I've had.

2020

First website, then no website

Fell for the idea that you can just build things. Shipped my first site, lost it to a dead hosting account, and learned about backups the expensive way.

2022

SPŠE Pardubice

Chose the school that would keep pushing me. Backend clicked here — Java first, then everything around it.

2024

Competitions and a jury to face

Hackathon wins, a national final, and the first time I had to explain my own architecture to strangers under a clock.

Now

Freelance and Prezz

Paid client work, a web dev group to help lead, and a production platform that people use without knowing my name. Exactly where I wanted to be at 19.
